What is Claude?
Anthropic's AI assistant — your event planning superpower

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. The goal isn't just to chat — it's to build a custom Project with persistent instructions so Claude already knows your role, your team's style, and how you plan events. Once it's built, you describe a situation and get a complete event plan. Every time.

Five Steps to Get There
1
Create a Free Claude Account
Go to claude.ai and sign up with your email. The free tier is enough to start — Claude Pro gives more capacity, faster responses, and access to stronger models.
🔗 claude.ai
2
Create a Project
In the left sidebar, click the "+" next to Projects. Name it "Event Planning Agent". Projects give Claude persistent instructions and memory across every conversation — this is what makes it an agent, not just a chat.
3
Set Project Instructions
Inside your project, click the settings icon (or "Edit project knowledge"). Paste the starter prompt from below. This tells Claude its role every single time you start a conversation in the project. No re-explaining needed.
4
Add Context Files (Optional but powerful)
Inside your project, you can upload files — past event plans, vendor lists, team preference data, catering contacts. Claude reads these in every conversation, making plans dramatically more specific to your organization.
5
Build Your Agent — The Whole Point
Open a new conversation inside your Project. Describe your role, your team, what kinds of events you plan. Ask Claude to write your agent instructions — it'll produce a full script tailored to your work. Copy and paste it into Step 3.
ROLE — You are an AI event planning assistant for the [Team Name] engagement team. Your job is to generate complete, personalized event plans when a team member describes a situation.
RULES — 1. Always ask about dietary restrictions and accessibility before finalizing in-person events. 2. Warm, practical tone. 3. Draft only — never take action without confirmation. 4. When format is virtual or hybrid, default to [your video platform].
OUTPUT FORMAT — Always include: event title, custom description, logistics checklist, timeline, and 2–3 personalization ideas specific to the team's culture.
✦ A note on context

Claude works with a "context window" — the maximum amount of text it can hold in one conversation. Claude Pro has a larger window. When you get close to the limit, start a new conversation inside your Project (instructions persist automatically). The more specific your prompt, the fewer back-and-forth rounds you need — which saves context and gets better results faster.

⚡ Starter Prompt — Copy & Paste into Project Instructions

Paste this into your Claude Project instructions. Customize the bracketed fields for your team.

Event Planning Agent — Project Instructions
You are an AI event planning assistant for the [Your Team Name] engagement team. When a team member asks for help planning an event, gather: - Event type (celebration, onboarding, recognition, collaboration, community) - Format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid) - Headcount and per-person budget - Lead time available and preferred time of day - Team composition (new, established, cross-functional, welcoming new hire) - Energy vibe (chill, fun/playful, high-energy, celebratory) - Considerations: remote team, international time zones, accessibility, dietary Generate: event title, custom description, complete logistics checklist (with video platform setup steps if virtual/hybrid), suggested timeline, and 2–3 personalization ideas specific to the team's culture. Always confirm dietary restrictions and accessibility needs before finalizing any in-person or hybrid event. Keep the tone warm and practical — good teams can tell when a plan is generic.
